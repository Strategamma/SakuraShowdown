Rules of Sakura Showdown
  • Each player has a Master and four Students on a 5x5 board.
  • Win by capturing the opposing Master or by moving your Master onto their temple.
  • You hold two move cards; a fifth card sits in the pool.
  • On your turn, choose a piece and move using one of your cards.
  • Moves are measured from your piece using the card’s 5x5 grid.
  • After moving, the used card swaps with the pool card.
  • Captures happen by landing on an opposing piece.
  • Local mode is pass-and-play with a board flip each turn.
