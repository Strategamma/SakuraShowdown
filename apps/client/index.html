How to play / Your first duel
Learn Sakura in three moves

You command one Dojo Master and four Disciples. Move with cards, capture opposing pieces, and protect your Master.

What you start with

5 piecesOne Master and four Disciples.
2 cardsEither card may move any of your pieces.
1 shared cardThe pool card changes after every move.

On your turn

Two ways to win

ACapture the rival MasterLand your Master or any Disciple directly on the opposing Master.
BEnter the rival templeMove your Master—not a Disciple—onto the marked temple square across the board.

How to read a movement card

The pink center dot is your selected piece. The brown squares are its possible destinations. Read every card from your side of the board—forward always points toward your opponent.

Important: cards describe a movement shape, not a specific piece. Either card can move your Master or any Disciple.

Playing together?Pass & Play tells you when to hand over the device and flips the board after each turn. On two phones, each player always sees their own pieces at the bottom.
